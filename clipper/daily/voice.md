# voice.md (SHAK REPLAY)

The voice profile every yt_agent skill reads. Setup copies it to `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md`. Written 26 Nov 2026
from the owner's instructions in RUNBOOK_GTA.md and LEARNINGS.md. Shak Replay has no presenter and no voiceover, so
"voice" here means the titles, the on-screen title boxes and cards, the descriptions and the pinned comment.

## Who I am talking to

One person: a 15-24-year-old in the US who watches Kai Cenat and IShowSpeed streams, never has five hours to watch a
whole marathon, and scrolls Shorts at night wanting the one moment everyone in chat lost it over.

## How I actually talk

Like a friend texting you the clip: names first, what happened, no setup. Short and loud.
- "Speed Lost It on Kai During Their $1M Race 😭"
- "Kai Cenat Got Humbled On GTA VI 😭" (a niche outlier, 3x its channel)
- "Kevin Hart COOKED Kai Cenat, Druski & AMP 😭" (a niche outlier, 7x its channel)
Title boxes on Shorts: names first, the setup not the punchline, `*one red word*`.

## Words I never use

"insane", "crazy", "best" (vague: name the thing instead), "you won't believe", "gone wrong", "must watch",
"part 1" when there is no part 2, any slur or sexual word, "GTA 6" as the topic of a title.

## Words I do use

The streamers' names (IShowSpeed / Speed, Kai Cenat / Kai, Fanum, Duke Dennis, AMP), one all-caps verb at most
(COOKED, SHOOK, LOST IT), real numbers from the stream ($524K, $1M, 5 hours), 😭 / 💀 (one emoji per title).

## What I will not claim

Nothing that did not happen in the clip. No fake quotes, no "fight" or "beef" when they were joking, no money figure
that was not said on stream, no "exclusive", no claim that a streamer endorsed or watched the channel. Every streamer is
credited with their twitch.tv link.

## My format

Shorts: 15-30 s (59 s max), the whole stream picture on a blurred copy, a white title box, captions, face zooms,
the owner's SFX. Long: 6-9 min "Top 10" countdown, no voiceover, a title card before every moment. No intro, no
"subscribe" pitch at the start, ad-safe, strong swears bleeped. Posts 2 Shorts + 1 long a day at US peak times.
