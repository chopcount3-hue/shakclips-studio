## 3 clearest findings
1. Every Short with a named streamer gets a ~1,000-view Shorts-feed test within 16 h. Only one (Speed, 18 s, 88.8% viewed) went past it, with a second wave on day 3 (1.8k → 3.0k).
2. Long videos are dead on arrival on this channel right now (2 and 1 views after 35 h and 11 h). The custom thumbnail can't help when there are no impressions.
3. Shorter is better so far: 18–24 s Shorts beat the 31–33 s ones on views (and Speed on retention).

## Recommendation
Keep the Shorts with the frame and popular creators, but make these changes:
1. **Swap the daily long video for a 3rd Short** (same 3 uploads/day on the free plan). Bring back one long video a week only once a Short breaks 10k, and pin/link it from that Short.
2. **Keep Shorts at 15–24 s, name-first titles, popular creators**, cutting to the payoff within 2 s. Favour Speed/xQc/Kai-tier moments that pass the copy check (Speed is the only breakout so far).
3. **Connect the vidIQ browser extension** so the next review gets Shorts stayed-to-watch and long-video CTR. This run had no access to either.

**Paid vidIQ plan for more uploads:** not worth it yet for uploads. Most Shorts stop at about 1k, so more of them per day won't change much until one format starts breaking out. Note: this run **used up the vidIQ credits** after 13 calls (video_stats for 5 videos failed). If the daily checks need to keep running, the credit limit, not the upload limit, is the more pressing reason to look at a paid tier.
