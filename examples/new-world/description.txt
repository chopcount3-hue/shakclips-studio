Four billion miles from the Sun, a world nobody knew about has been drifting through the dark. It's called 2026 ED138, it may be up to 930 miles (1,500 km) wide, and it has been sitting in telescope images since at least 2016.

How far away is it, how big is it really, how did everyone miss it, and what would you see if you stood on it? We go out there, past Neptune and Pluto, to find out.

💬 Should a world this far out be called a planet, a dwarf planet, or something else? Tell me in the comments.
