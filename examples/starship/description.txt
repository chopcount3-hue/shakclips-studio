SpaceX already catches its giant Super Heavy booster with a pair of tower arms nicknamed "the chopsticks." On Starship Flight 15, set for no earlier than Friday, November 13, it wants to catch the spaceship on top too. No Starship upper stage has ever been caught.

Here is the fast, fact-checked explainer: the launch plan, how the booster catch works, the ship's almost-full-day trip around the Earth, the belly-flop and flip that ends between the chopsticks, what happens if something goes wrong, and why SpaceX wants to catch rockets at all.
