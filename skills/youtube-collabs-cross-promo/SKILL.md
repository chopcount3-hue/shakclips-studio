# YouTube Collabs & Cross-Promo (Shak Clips)

Use when looking for partner channels, pitching or running a collaboration, swapping shout-outs, or sharing the channel in outside communities.
Legend: **[V]** = checked on an official page on 2026-11-11 (see Sources). **[H]** = heuristic, source named. **[A]** = a planning assumption to replace with real numbers.

## 0. What we bring at 7 subscribers (honest)
- A bigger channel gains almost nothing from our reach. Our currency is the **engine**: we can animate their story in our style, fast. So every pitch offers a finished asset, never "let's grow together".
- Partner size: active channels with 200–20,000 subscribers in adjacent niches (history, science, explainers, teachers, podcasts) **[A]**. Skip channels that haven't uploaded in 30 days.
- Collabs add to the daily run. They never replace it, and they never skip its quality gate.

## 1. YouTube's official tools (as of 2026-11-11)
| Tool | What it does | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Collaborations** **[V]** | Invite up to 10 creators onto one video. Each shows with a subscribe button on desktop, mobile and TV, and YouTube aims to recommend the video to every collaborator's audience | Long videos, Shorts and archived lives (not active lives). The invitee must accept. **Revenue goes only to the posting channel, not split.** Eligibility isn't stated, so if "Collaboration" is missing at upload, it isn't on for us yet |
| Shorts Remix → **Collab** / Green Screen **[V]** | Build a Short alongside, or on top of, another Short or video. The player credits and links the source | The source can opt out. Ours is opted in by default (Studio → Content → video → Show more → Shorts remixing) |
| End screen **Channel** and **Video** elements **[V]** | "Promote another channel with a custom message", or feature another channel's public or unlisted video | Last 5–20 s, up to 4 elements, video at least 25 s long |
| Collaborative playlists **[V]** | Invitees add videos and can remove only their own | The playlist must be public or unlisted |
| Home tab "Channels" section **[V]** | Highlights featured channels | Up to 12 sections on the Home tab |
| Shorts related video **[V]** | Links a Short to a video **from your own channel** | Can't point at a partner, so tag them as a collaborator instead |
| Go Live Together **[V]** | One mobile guest at a time; the host earns the revenue and answers for the guest's conduct | Skip: we don't stream live |
- Invite at upload **[V]**: Studio → Create → Upload videos → Details → under Audience, **Show more** → Collaboration → **Invite a collaborator**.

## 2. Finding partners (weekly, 20 min, free)
1. Start from the small channels already in the `youtube-competitor-outliers` list.
2. Add more from three places: YouTube search on this week's topics (filter: this month), the "Channels" sections of those channels, and commenters on our niche's videos who run channels.
3. Score each 1–5 on five points; keep only 18/25 or better **[A]**:
   - audience fit (English, US-leaning);
   - accuracy (sources cited, no misinformation);
   - activity (uploaded in the last 30 days);
   - reciprocity (we can give them something they want);
   - advertiser-safe tone.
4. Red flags, never partner: sub4sub talk, views far below subscriber numbers (possibly bought), medical or conspiracy misinformation, edited real faces, crypto or gambling promos.
5. Log it in `collabs.md`: channel, id, subs, score, contact route, status, next date.

## 3. Formats that fit an animated explainer channel
| Format | How it works | Who posts | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Animated by Shak Clips"** | We animate 45–90 s of *their* narration or story in stick figures | They post, with us as collaborator. We post a Short cut with permission | Written OK to use their audio; their real face is never drawn as a likeness or edited |
| **Guest narrator** | They record one top-5 entry or a counter-"take" in their own voice | We post; they're the collaborator | Real voice, recorded by them. Never clone a voice |
| **Joint top-5** | Same theme, two separate original videos; each ends teasing the other's #1 | Both | No re-uploads of each other's footage. Add the end-screen Video element |
| **Answer their question** | A question from their community post or comments becomes our explainer | We post; they're the collaborator | Credit the asker; 2-source fact rule |
| **Shorts Collab remix** | A 20–40 s animated explanation beside a niche Short | Us | Only where remixing is allowed. Add facts, not just a reaction (reused-content rule) |
| **Joint playlist** | 3–5 small channels co-curate "best animated history" | Shared | Each adds its own best videos |

## 4. Outreach: short, specific, no spam
- At most 5 personalised messages a day **[A]**, sent only to the business email in their About section or another public contact route.
- One follow-up after 7 days, then stop. No DM blasts.
- No "check out my channel" comments: identical messages like that across many videos are named as comment spam **[V]**.
- Say up front that our narration is an AI voice.
- **A. "Animated by" pitch** (subject: `animated version of your <video>?`)
  > Hi <name>, your video "<title>" had the best take I've seen on <specific point>. I run Shak Clips, a small stick-figure explainer channel. I'd like to animate 60 s of your narration from it, free, for you to post as a collab with both channels tagged. Here's a 20 s sample in our style: <unlisted link>. Want me to make it? — <owner>, Shak Clips
- **B. Guest narrator** (subject: `your voice on a top-5?`)
  > Hi <name>, I'm making "<top-5 title>" for <date>, and your work on <topic> makes you the right voice for #<n>. It's about 45 s of script; I send the text, you record it on your phone, I animate it and add you as collaborator. Interested?
- **C. Follow-up, once**
  > Hi <name>, following up once on the <format> idea. If it's not a fit, no problem, and thanks for <specific video>.
- Never promise views or subscribers. Never offer money or gifts for subscribers.

## 5. Shout-out swaps vs banned sub4sub
| Allowed | Banned |
|---|---|
| An honest recommendation of a channel we watch: end-screen Channel element, Home "Channels" section, a pinned-comment shout-out | **Sub4sub**: subscribing only if they subscribe back, and coordinated sub4sub schemes **[V]** |
| Collaborator tags on genuine joint videos **[V]** | Buying views or subs; linking to metric-gaming services **[V]** |
| Asking viewers to subscribe, like, share or comment **[V]** | Rewards for views, likes or comments; misleading cards **[V]** |
| A swap disclosed in plain words ("we're trading shout-outs this week") **[H]** | View, like or comment exchange groups. Violations by promoters you hire count against you **[V]** |
- If money, free products or free animation work changes hands, it's paid promotion:
  - Tick Studio → Content → video → **paid promotion** **[V]**.
  - Also say it in the video, aloud and on screen **[V]** (FTC; US law applies when US viewers are reasonably foreseeable).
  - Avoid vague labels like "collab" alone **[V]**.

## 6. Communities that allow self-promotion
- Read each community's rules before posting. Give value first and say "I made this". The Reddit rules and the 9:1 habit are in `youtube-repurposing-distribution` §4.
- Usually OK **[H]**:
  - designated self-promo threads in creator subreddits and creator Discords;
  - animation communities that welcome original work with process notes;
  - teacher groups, for free classroom material rather than view-chasing.
- Never post the same link in several communities on the same day. A community that bans links gets a text answer only.

## 7. Running a collab (checklist)
- [ ] Email agreement covering:
  - who makes what, by when, and who posts;
  - the credit line;
  - that each side keeps its own audio and footage, with a licence to use the agreed clips in Shorts and our portfolio;
  - that either side can ask for removal;
  - whether anything is paid (if so, disclose it).
- [ ] Their claims pass our 2-source rule; allegations say "alleged" or "reportedly".
- [ ] Collaborator invite sent at upload, and accepted.
- [ ] Description credit with a link; a pinned comment thanking them; an end-screen Channel element.
- [ ] "Narration: AI voice (Kokoro TTS)" in the description.

## 8. Tracking and scale/kill rules
- Add a `collabs.md` row 7 and 28 days after each collab:
  - date, partner, format;
  - our views and subscribers gained;
  - the partner video's public view count;
  - returning-viewer change.
- Traffic-source reading follows `youtube-analytics-loop`.
- The collaborations help page doesn't describe separate analytics for collab videos **[V]**, so compare each one with our median video.
- Scale: a format that brings 2× our median subscribers per video **[A]** gets repeated with 2 new partners.
- Kill: 3 collabs in one format at or below the median → drop the format. Don't judge before day 7.
