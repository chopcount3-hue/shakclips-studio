# YouTube Community Engagement (Shak Clips)

Use when setting up or reviewing how viewers move from one video to the next, how comments are handled, or what to post between uploads.
Legend: **[V]** = checked on an official page (see Sources). **[H]** = heuristic (unverified).

## 0. Unlock the tools first (blocker)
- **Pinned comments, chapters, clickable description links and a Short's related-video link all need advanced features.** Applying for monetization needs them too **[V]**.
- Custom thumbnails need intermediate features, i.e. phone verification **[V]**. vidIQ refused the custom thumbnail on 2026-11-10, so do this first:
  1. Phone verification at m.youtube.com/verify.
  2. ID or video verification (review usually takes about 24 h) **[V]**.
  3. Check the result in Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility.

## 1. The loop to build
Short → related-video link → long video → end screen → series playlist → subscribe → bell.
- Track it weekly in Studio's Audience tab: **returning viewers** (watched before and came back) vs **new viewers** **[V]**. Target: returning viewers rising week over week.
- Short → long: in Studio → Content → the Short → Related video, pick its long video **[V]** (public or unlisted, from your own channel). It can be set after upload.
  - The vidIQ upload doesn't set this, so it's a 1-minute daily owner task until it can be automated.

## 2. Pinned comment (every long video, within 1 h of 22:00 UTC)
- Only one comment can be pinned; pinning a new one unpins the old **[V]**.
- Post from the channel account in the Studio app, then pin it. Your own links are never held by the link filter **[V]**.
- Rotate these templates and rewrite them for each video, never pasting the same text:
  - **Take:** "Shak's take: <the video's sharpest opinion in 1 line>. Agree, or what am I missing? Best counter-point gets a heart and a shout-out on Saturday."
  - **Accuracy:** "Every claim has its source in the description. Spot an error? Reply here; I'll fix it and credit you."
  - **Next step:** "If this one hooked you, <series> continues <day> with <teaser>. The playlist is linked on the end screen."

## 3. Replies: who does it (the owner is busy)
| Option | Cost | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Owner, Studio app, 10 min at about 08:00 IST (02:30 UTC, 9:30 PM ET, ~4.5 h after publish). Filter Response status = Unresponded **[V]** | free | default, daily |
| A trusted helper with the **Editor (limited)** role: can comment, can't see revenue **[V]** | free | if the owner can't keep up |
| Claude drafts replies from comments the owner pastes in | free | a batch of 10–20 tricky ones |
| vidIQ: read comments 5 credits, draft replies 1, post a reply 1 (posting only after the owner approves the exact text) | credits | after 2026-12-08, at most one ~30-credit weekly batch; keep the other credits for analytics |

- Priority: factual questions and corrections → first-time commenters → civil disagreement → praise (a heart is enough).
- Never auto-post generic replies. Comments made by automatic systems to inflate metrics break the fake-engagement policy **[V]**.
- "50+ replies in the first 2 h lifts reach 15–20%" is a repo claim, **[H]**. Don't plan around it.

## 4. Posts and polls (between uploads)
- Eligibility **[V]**: no subscriber minimum is listed. Posts are unavailable for Made-for-Kids channels and supervised accounts.
- Post types: text, image (up to 10 images), polls (up to 4 answers of 65 characters or fewer), quizzes (4 answers, one correct) and video **[V]**.
- Posts can be scheduled, and there is a per-24 h limit (number not stated) **[V]**.
- Weekly batch (schedule it all on Sunday, 15 min, in YouTube or the Studio app):
  - **Mon:** poll, "Which story is #1 in Saturday's Top 5?", with 4 real options.
  - **Wed:** quiz from Tuesday's true story, with the answer explained.
  - **Fri:** image post, a frame from Thursday's what-if plus one question.
- Posts also count as channel activity. YPP can turn off monetization after 6 months with no uploads or posts **[V]**.

## 5. Hype (not available yet)
- **[V]** (answer/15509925): Hype requires YPP **and** 500–500,000 subscribers, with the creator in a listed country (India and the US are both listed).
- Only long videos in their **first 7 days** can be hyped; Shorts can't. Misleading or clickbait videos, Made-for-Kids videos and videos with copyright claims are ineligible.
- Hype is on by default for eligible channels. Weekly country leaderboards show the most-hyped videos.
- The official page gives **no** free-hypes-per-week number and **no** small-channel bonus, so treat those repo claims as unverified.
- Once in YPP: one short Hype ask in the outro of the strongest video each week (never in Shorts). Check the leaderboard on Sundays.

## 6. End screens and cards
- End screens **[V]**:
  - Shown in the last 5–20 s; the video must be at least 25 s long.
  - Up to 4 elements on 16:9. The link element needs YPP.
  - Unavailable on Made-for-Kids videos. The watermark and card teasers are hidden while the end screen shows.
- Default layout: (1) the next video in the same series, (2) that series' playlist, (3) subscribe.
- The script's last 15–20 s should be a bridge line that names the next video, over a clean frame (the runbook's end card).
- Cards **[V]**: up to 5 per video, shown only to viewers on computers, link cards need YPP. Use 1–2 cards exactly when the narration mentions an older video.

## 7. Playlists, trailer, featured video
- One playlist per series (Shak Explains / True Story Tuesday / What If Thursday / Top 5 Saturday). Add each video the day it goes live.
- "Series playlist" needs a verified account, and a video can be in only one series playlist **[V]**.
- Order: evergreen playlists best-first, trending newest-first **[H]**.
- Channel trailer (shown to non-subscribers, never again once watched) **[V]**:
  - Length 45–60 s **[H]**: the best hook, then "what you get, when", then the schedule line.
  - Re-cut it each quarter from the top performer.
- Spotlight / featured video for returning subscribers **[V]**: always the newest evergreen video.

## 8. Subscribe prompts that don't hurt retention
- Asking to subscribe, like or comment is allowed. Rewards for engagement and sub4sub are banned **[V]**.
- Shape of the ask **[H]**:
  - Nothing in the first 30 s.
  - One ask of 6 s or less right after the first payoff (40–60% of the way in), tied to a promise ("Every Tuesday, a true story that sounds fake. Subscribe so you don't miss one.").
  - One in the outro. No "smash that".
- Check Studio's retention graph: if a dip lines up with the ask, move or shorten it.

## 9. Negative comments and spam
- Settings **[V]**:
  - "Hold potentially inappropriate comments" set to Strict.
  - Blocked words: held comments are kept up to 60 days.
  - "Hide user" removes a commenter's comments from the whole channel.
- Starter blocked-words list **[H]**, aimed at impersonation scams: telegram, whatsapp, wa.me, t.me, "contact me", crypto, forex, investment, giveaway.
- Accuracy complaint: check it within 24 h.
  - If the viewer is right, fix the description, pin a correction, thank them, and note it in `log.md`.
  - If they're wrong, reply once with the source.
- Political heat: answer factual questions only and stay neutral (runbook rule). Never argue in threads.
- Harassment or hate: hide the user and report. Leave civil criticism up; deleting it looks worse.
