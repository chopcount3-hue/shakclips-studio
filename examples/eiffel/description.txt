TITLE:
The Con Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower (And Got Away With It)

DESCRIPTION:
In 1925, a man in a perfect suit walked into one of the finest hotels in Paris… and sold the Eiffel Tower to a scrap metal dealer. Then he came back and tried to sell it again.

This is the true story of "Count" Victor Lustig: the fake government title, the secret meeting at the Hôtel de Crillon, the bribe that made it all feel real, his con on Al Capone, and his escape on a rope made of bedsheets.
