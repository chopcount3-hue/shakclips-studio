# Clip packaging (YouTube + Facebook)

## Shorts title (under 60 characters)
- Formula: **[Streamer] + what happened, with emotion**. Then 1 emoji and 2–3 hashtags.
  - "Kai Cenat vs the mountain 😭 #kaicenat #gta6 #shorts"
  - "Speed got 5 stars in 10 seconds 💀 #ishowspeed #gta6"
- The streamer's name always comes first; it's what people search for. Spell it exactly (Kai Cenat, IShowSpeed, Fanum).
- Never claim something that isn't in the clip. No "LEAKED", no "FIRST LOOK" before 19 Nov.

## Description (every upload)
```
<one-line hook>
🎮 Original stream: <streamer> — <link to the VOD or channel>
All credit to <streamer>. Clipped with permission / under their clipping program.
Edited by <channel> — captions, sound design and replays added.
#gta6 #<streamer> #gta #shorts #funny
```
The credit line is required by most clipping programs, and it also helps the "own edit" case under YouTube's reused-content rules.

## Long-form title and thumbnail
- Title: "GTA 6 Streamers' FUNNIEST Moments (Day 3) — Kai Cenat, Speed, Fanum". Day numbers make it a series.
- Thumbnail: 1280×720. The best reaction face cut out large on one side (from the stream frame; the streamer's own content,
  credited), the game moment on the other, 2–3 words in 3D yellow ("HE DID WHAT?"). Readable at 320×180. Build it with the
  Shak Clips thumbnail builder approach (`../skills/youtube-thumbnail-design`).

## Facebook
- Reels: the same vertical file. The caption is the title plus the credit line; 3–5 hashtags.
- Long-form: the same 16:9 file. Facebook favours 3+ minutes for in-stream ads.
- Post times: stagger 30–60 min after YouTube.

## Hashtag pool
`#gta6 #gtavi #gta #<streamer> #twitch #streamer #funny #fails #shorts #gaming`, at most 5 per upload.
