# YouTube Monetization & Revenue (Shak Clips)

Use when checking YPP progress, preparing the application, deciding what's safe to cover, placing mid-rolls, or planning income beyond ads.
Legend: **[V]** = checked on an official page (see Sources). **[H]** = heuristic (unverified). Money figures marked [H] are planning guesses, not promises.

## 1. The bars (as of 2026-11-10)
| Tier | Requirement | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Fan-funding tier **[V]** | 500 subs + 3 public uploads in 90 days + (3,000 h in 12 months **or** 3M Shorts views in 90 days) | Super Thanks, Super Chat, memberships, Shopping. **Unchanged after 1 Feb 2027** |
| Ads, until 31 Jan 2027 **[V]** | 1,000 subs + (4,000 qualified public long-form hours in 12 months **or** 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days) | watch-page ads, Premium revenue |
| Ads, new applicants from 1 Feb 2027 **[V]** | 1,000 subs + (8,000 h in 365 days **or** 20M Shorts views in 90 days) | same. Existing partners aren't affected |

- What counts **[V]**: Shorts-feed views never count toward hours. Hours from private, unlisted or deleted videos don't count, so fix a weak video rather than deleting it.
- Also needed **[V]**: 2-Step Verification, **advanced features**, no active strikes, one linked AdSense for YouTube account, residence in a YPP country, and following the monetization policies.
- Review and appeals **[V]**: review takes about 1 month. Appeal within 21 days; reapply after 30 days (90 days after a second rejection).
- Terms **[V]**: partners must accept the updated YPP terms in Studio by 31 Jan 2027.
- Pending applications: the official pages **don't say** how applications pending on 1 Feb are judged. "Apply by 31 Jan and keep the old bar" is unverified, so only Creator Support can confirm it.

## 2. Pace math (every Sunday, free: `vidiq_user_channels` + Studio → Earn)
- Hours per day needed = (4,000 − hours now) ÷ days to 24 Jan 2027 (a 7-day buffer). From 0 hours on 10 Nov, that's 75 days and **53.3 h/day**.
- Long-video views per day = hours per day × 60 ÷ average view duration in minutes. At 3:00 AVD **[H]**, that's about 1,070 views/day.
- Subs per day = (1,000 − subs) ÷ days left. From 7 subs, about 13/day.
- Decision rule: if on **10 Jan 2027** the projected hours at 24 Jan are still below 4,000, drop the deadline.
  - New target: the fan-funding tier first (the 3M Shorts path), then the 8,000 h bar.
  - Never chase the deadline with spam, bought views or misleading titles. They ruin the review and add nothing.
- The day subs reach 1,000 with hours met, apply the same day in Studio → Earn (the runbook already notifies the owner).

## 3. Before applying (do now)
1. Phone verification, then ID or video verification, to get advanced features **[V]**.
2. 2-Step Verification on the Google account.
3. Run `youtube-channel-branding-audit` and score 18/20 or better. Reviewers look at the main theme, most-viewed videos, newest videos, the biggest watch-time share, the metadata and the About section **[V]**.
4. Audience setting: "No, not made for kids". It's accurate for news and scandals, and Made for Kids disables Super Thanks, memberships, end screens and posts **[V]**.

## 4. AdSense and US tax for an India-based creator
- Link one AdSense for YouTube account. The payout threshold is **$100** **[V]**. Address verification is triggered at $10 **[V]**, so keep the postal address current.
- US tax info goes in AdSense for YouTube → Payments → Payments info → Manage settings → Payments profile → United States tax info **[V]**.
- Withholding on an individual's earnings from US viewers **[V]**:
  - **15%** with tax info and the India treaty claimed.
  - 30% with tax info but no treaty claim.
  - **Up to 24% of total worldwide earnings** if no tax info is given.
- Worked example for this US-heavy channel ($1,000 total, $800 of it from US viewers): $120 withheld with the treaty vs $240 with no tax info.
- Timing **[V]**: submit or renew by **10 Dec** each year. Forms expire at the end of the 3rd full calendar year. Form 1042-S is issued by 14 Apr.
- The tax tool picks the form (usually W-8BEN for individuals **[H]**). For Indian income tax and GST on foreign income, see a chartered accountant (not covered here).

## 5. Mid-roll ads (long videos, once monetized)
- Mid-rolls need a monetized video of **8:00 or longer** **[V]**. Aim for 8:30 or more so a trim doesn't cross the line.
- Slot types **[V]**: manual, automatic (the default for new uploads) or both. Slots at pauses or visual transitions are likelier to serve. Studio marks slots that interrupt speech in red.
- Pipeline tie-in: end each act on its open loop, then a 0.6–1.0 s silent beat with a scene change. Log the act-break timestamps from the sentence timings and place manual slots there.
- Spacing: about 1 slot per 2–3 minutes, none in the first 60 s **[H]**. "Mid-rolls roughly double RPM" is **[H]**.

## 6. Advertiser-friendly rules for news, scandal and politics (avoiding the yellow icon) [V]
- **Titles and thumbnails are judged too, and often more strictly.**
  - Moderate profanity in a title or thumbnail gives limited ads; strong profanity gives no ads. Keep zero profanity anywhere.
- Violence and tragedy:
  - News or educational context can keep full ads.
  - Graphic detail of multi-casualty events gives limited ads.
  - Exploiting a sensitive event, or using its keywords to pull traffic, gives no ads.
- Controversial issues: non-graphic news coverage is fine; a graphic thumbnail drops to limited ads.
- Shaming, malicious personal attacks or defamation give limited or no ads. That's why allegations stay "alleged" or "reportedly", and each side's response is given.
- False claims about voting procedures or election results give no ads. Neutral public-interest coverage of politics is fine.
- Scandal pre-flight **[H]**:
  - No "EXPOSED / DESTROYED / CAUGHT" in titles.
  - No weapons or blood in thumbnails.
  - Real people only as neutral stick figures.
  - The thumbnail shows only what the video shows.
- If a video gets a yellow icon, request human review **[V]**. Log the topic type so the calendar avoids repeats.

## 7. Shorts money (mostly a growth tool here)
- Pool mechanics **[V]**: Shorts-feed ad revenue is pooled.
  - Music shrinks the pool: with 1 licensed track, half of that Short's revenue goes to the pool; with 2 tracks, one third.
  - Creators keep **45%** of their allocation, based on engaged views only. Non-original Shorts don't count.
- **From 1 Feb 2027, Shorts ad and Premium revenue needs 10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days** **[V]**. Below that, the channel stays in YPP but Shorts earn nothing.
- The Shorts bonus program is invite-only **[V]**.
- Keep the engine's original music. A Content ID claim on a Short over 1 minute blocks it worldwide **[V]**.

## 8. Other revenue (unlock in order)
- **Super Thanks, Super Chat, memberships** **[V]**:
  - 70% of net revenue to the creator. Requirements: age 18+ and the Commerce Product Module.
  - Super Thanks works on long videos and Shorts, India and the US are both listed, and it's off if comments are off.
  - Memberships only make sense once there's a perk worth paying for, e.g. early access or a members-only "Shak's take" **[H]**.
- **YouTube Shopping affiliate** **[V]**: needs YPP; India and the US are listed.
  - Merchants set commission rates. Payouts come through AdSense 60–120 days after purchase.
  - There's a temporary 100% commission incentive, with at least 30 days' notice before it ends.
- **Affiliate links** (e.g. book links for history videos) need clickable description links, i.e. advanced features **[V]**.
- **Sponsorships**: tick "paid promotion" in Studio **[V]**, and also disclose in the video itself, in both audio and on screen. Platform tools alone aren't enough, and US FTC rules apply to posts that reach US consumers **[V]**.
- Sponsorship rates are **[H]**:
  - About $10–$30 per 1,000 expected views.
  - The repo claims $1K–$1.5K per video at 5K–15K average views.
  - Don't pitch before about 5K average views per long video. Refuse crypto, gambling and guaranteed-returns finance.
- **Digital products** **[H]**: "teacher packs" (worksheet, timeline and quiz per history video) at $3–$9 via Gumroad or Payhip, once links are clickable.

## 9. Roadmap: 0 → 1k → YPP → beyond
| Stage | Trigger | Actions | Income |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | now | verification, tax prep, end screens, 8:30+ videos, ads-safe topics | $0 |
| Fan funding | 500 subs + 3,000 h or 3M Shorts | apply, turn on Super Thanks | $ (tips) |
| Ads | 1,000 subs + 4,000 h by 31 Jan (else 8,000 h) | AdSense + treaty tax form, mid-rolls on every long video | ads |
| Grow RPM | in YPP | Hype asks, Shopping, Q4 evergreen push **[H]**, sponsor media kit at 5K views | ads + extras |
| Diversify | 10K subs **[H]** | memberships, teacher packs, a second language track | multiple |
- RPM planning **[H]**: the repo lists Education at $8–15 for US audiences. Plan at $3–6 for a news and history mix until Studio shows real numbers.
